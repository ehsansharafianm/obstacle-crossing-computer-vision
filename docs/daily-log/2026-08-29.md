# 2026-08-29 — Obstacle detection fixed; multi-height capture works

Pushed the pipeline through two multi-height obstacle sessions (test13: 2 heights,
test14: 4 configs) and fixed the three things that stood between us and clean
per-time obstacle positions at every height. See [[progress-log]] and [[next-steps]].

## Headline
- **test14 (4 obstacle configs, 5 min, ~15 crossings): obstacle reconstructed in 75 %
  of frames**, all four windows resolved with **sub-mm scatter** (IQR e.g. 355–355 mm).
  Feet 15 mm / 13 mm toe-heel std across every crossing.
- The obstacle went from **6 % coverage with 550 mm spikes → 75 % clean flat tracks**.

## What we fixed (in order)

### 1. Obstacle outlier filter (from test13)
- `match_reds` now returns its mean reprojection error; the obstacle loop drops frames
  above ~28 px (a foot occluding a red → gross mismatch).
- Two-pass **MAD rolling-median** filter (~1 s window, thr = max(30 mm, 4·MAD)) removes
  residual spikes the old fixed-window median let through.

### 2. cam3 clap sync was landing on a gait-period alias
- Symptom: `cam3 sync +5.740s [clap OFF]` — the rigid-shoe arbiter found a lower-scatter
  but *wrong* offset one stride away from the clap (+7.737 s).
- Fix: the clap is a **hard physical anchor**. When the rigid minimum disagrees with the
  clap by >1 s, trust the clap unless its own local fit is much worse (bad detection).
- Result: `cam3 sync +7.657s [clap agrees]`, cam2↔cam3 scatter 24 → 15 mm, cam3 gap-fill
  4846 → 5308 points, **R-foot outliers 1001 → 88**.

### 3. Obstacle detection — the real bottleneck
- **The reds were barely being detected**: each camera saw 2 reds in only 11/27/6 % of
  frames, so whole time-windows had zero obstacle data.
- Root cause: the balls are **orange-red (hue ≈ 3–9)** but the detector used hue 172–180
  only (to dodge skin). It caught them by luck in some frames, missed them in others.
- Naively widening to low hue then grabbed the **person's bare legs** and orange
  equipment (same hue, big blobs).
- Fix (in `occ.tracking`):
  - `red_ground` extended to hue **0–12 and 170–180**, high saturation floor; upper
    bound 12 stays below the **orange support poles** (hue 16–25).
  - New **`detect_round_blobs`** shape gate (circularity 4πA/P² ≥ 0.55, bbox fill ≥ 0.6):
    the round balls pass (circ 0.8–0.9); elongated legs / irregular equipment (circ 0.2)
    are rejected. Validated on all 3 cameras across the whole timeline before re-tracking.
- **Also gave the obstacle the feet's n-view treatment**: `match_reds_nview` reconstructs
  each red from *any* 2 of the 3 cameras (was cam1+cam2 only).
- Result: obstacle coverage **6 % → 75 %**, four configs cleanly separated.

## Bottleneck / note for the 6-height protocol
- Detection now works with the orange-red balls, but they are **the fragile part** of the
  scene (share hue with skin + poles). A marker colour that is *absent* from the room
  (e.g. blue) would make the obstacle bulletproof — worth considering, not required.
- Processing is ~55 min for a 5-min 3-camera session (tracking dominates); the detection
  cache makes re-runs after a tuning change ~40 s.
